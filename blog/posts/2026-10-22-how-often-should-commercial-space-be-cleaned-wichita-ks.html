How Often Should a Commercial Space Be Cleaned?

Published October 22, 2026

One of the most common questions Wichita business owners ask is how often their office or commercial space should be cleaned. There is no single answer, but there are clear guidelines based on your type of business, foot traffic, and goals. Here is how to find the right cleaning frequency for your space.

1. Factors That Determine Cleaning Frequency

  • Foot traffic: More customers and employees means more cleaning.
  • Type of business: Clinics, restaurants, and gyms need more frequent service than a quiet office.
  • Size of your team: More people means more restroom use, trash, and breakroom activity.
  • Flooring type: Carpet and hard floors need different levels of maintenance.
  • Brand image: Customer-facing spaces should stay consistently presentable.

2. General Guidelines by Business Type

Small offices often do well with service two to three times per week. Busy offices and customer-facing businesses typically benefit from daily cleaning. Medical offices and high-traffic spaces usually need daily service with extra attention to restrooms and high-touch surfaces. Low-traffic spaces may only need weekly service.

3. Do Not Forget Periodic Deep Cleaning

Routine cleaning keeps your space tidy, but some tasks are best handled on a monthly, quarterly, or annual schedule. These include carpet deep cleaning, floor stripping and waxing, window cleaning, and high dusting. Adding periodic deep cleaning to your plan protects your floors and keeps your space looking its best.

4. Start Somewhere and Adjust

The best cleaning schedule is one that fits your needs and budget. Many businesses start with a reasonable baseline and adjust as they learn what works. A good cleaning company will review your schedule with you and make changes as your business grows.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

How often should a small office be cleaned?

Many small offices do well with cleaning two to three times per week, with restrooms and trash handled each visit. Offices with heavy foot traffic may need daily service.

How often should carpets be deep cleaned in a commercial space?

Carpets in commercial spaces are commonly deep cleaned a few times per year, and more often in high-traffic areas.

Can I change my cleaning schedule later?

Yes. A good cleaning company will adjust your schedule as your business, staff size, or needs change.
