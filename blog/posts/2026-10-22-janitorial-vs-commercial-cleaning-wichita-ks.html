Janitorial Services vs. Commercial Cleaning in Wichita, KS

Published October 22, 2026

If you are shopping for a cleaning company in Wichita, you will see the terms "janitorial services" and "commercial cleaning" used everywhere. Are they the same thing? Not exactly. Here is a simple breakdown so you know what to ask for and what your business actually needs.

1. What Are Janitorial Services?

Janitorial services focus on routine, recurring maintenance that keeps a building tidy day to day. This usually includes emptying trash, cleaning and restocking restrooms, vacuuming, mopping, and wiping down surfaces. Janitorial service is typically scheduled daily or several times a week.

2. What Is Commercial Cleaning?

Commercial cleaning is a broader term. It includes routine cleaning but often extends to deeper, specialized work such as carpet extraction, floor stripping and waxing, window cleaning, and detailed sanitizing. Commercial cleaning is often scheduled periodically to restore and protect your space.

3. Key Differences at a Glance

  • Frequency: Janitorial is ongoing and routine. Commercial deep cleaning is often periodic.
  • Scope: Janitorial covers day-to-day upkeep. Commercial cleaning can include specialized projects.
  • Equipment: Deep cleaning tasks often require specialized machines and products.

4. Which Does Your Wichita Business Need?

Most businesses benefit from both. Routine janitorial service keeps your office, shop, or clinic presentable every day, while periodic deep cleaning keeps carpets, floors, and surfaces in top shape for the long run. The best approach is to work with one reliable company that can handle both under a clear, customized plan.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

Is janitorial service the same as commercial cleaning?

The terms overlap and are often used interchangeably. Janitorial usually means routine maintenance, while commercial cleaning can also include deeper, specialized work.

Can one company provide both janitorial and commercial cleaning services?

Yes. Many Wichita cleaning companies offer both routine janitorial service and periodic deep cleaning under one agreement.

Which service does my business need?

Most businesses need a mix. Routine service keeps your space tidy day to day, and periodic deep cleaning restores floors, carpets, and surfaces.
