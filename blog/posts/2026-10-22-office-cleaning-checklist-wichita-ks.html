Office Cleaning Checklist for Wichita, KS Businesses

Published October 22, 2026

A clean office makes a strong first impression, keeps employees healthier, and helps your business run smoothly. But what should a professional office cleaning service in Wichita, KS actually be doing every visit? Use this commercial cleaning checklist to set clear expectations and hold your cleaning company accountable.

1. Daily Office Cleaning Tasks

  • Empty trash and recycling bins and replace liners
  • Vacuum carpets and mop hard floors in high-traffic areas
  • Clean, disinfect, and restock restrooms
  • Wipe down breakroom counters, tables, and appliance exteriors
  • Disinfect high-touch points like door handles, light switches, and elevator buttons
  • Dust and wipe reception desks and common areas

2. Weekly Cleaning Tasks

  • Dust vents, ledges, and window sills
  • Clean glass doors and interior windows
  • Detail-clean desks and workstations as requested
  • Spot-clean walls and door frames
  • Vacuum edges and under furniture

3. Monthly and Periodic Deep Cleaning

  • Carpet deep cleaning or extraction
  • Floor scrubbing, buffing, and waxing
  • High dusting of light fixtures and ceiling corners
  • Upholstery cleaning for chairs and waiting areas

4. Why Your Checklist Should Be Customized

No two offices are the same. A law firm, a call center, and a medical office all have different needs, flooring, and traffic patterns. The best commercial cleaning companies in Wichita build a custom plan around your space, your schedule, and your budget instead of using a generic one-size-fits-all list.

5. Frequently Asked Questions

What should be included in a daily office cleaning?

Daily service typically covers trash removal, restroom cleaning and restocking, vacuuming, breakroom wipe-downs, and disinfecting frequently touched surfaces.

Should an office cleaning checklist be customized?

Yes. Every office has different flooring, layouts, and needs, so a good cleaning company will build a checklist around your specific space and schedule.

How do I know my cleaning company is following the checklist?

Ask for a written scope of work, request periodic walkthroughs, and choose a company that communicates openly and responds quickly to feedback.
